abstract: >-
  This study developed an objective, automated framework for assessing toddlers' gross motor development from behavioral videos. A graph convolutional network evaluated four validated motor behaviors from pose keypoints, and an XGBoost model integrated behavior-level predictions to assess overall gross motor status, achieving an AUROC of 0.90 while identifying key behaviors, moments, and body parts important for developmental assessment.
